Privacy Policy

This application is for personal use only and has a single user: its owner.

It accesses the owner's own Google Drive through the Google Drive API solely to synchronize files between Google Drive and the owner's own computer.

No data is collected, stored on any server, shared with, or sold to any third party. All data stays between Google Drive and the owner's device. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
